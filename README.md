# CurioQuest

An interactive learning app that turns children’s curiosity into engaging questions, quizzes, and rewards.

---

## 🚨 Problem
An 11-year-old child using a tablet spends hours on games and passive content, while educational apps fail to keep their attention because lessons are not interactive or based on what they actually want to learn.

---

## 💡 Solution
CurioQuest allows children to ask questions and receive simple explanations, followed by quizzes that reward them with points, while a smart contract securely tracks their learning progress.

---

## ⏱ Timeline
- Day 1: Smart contract development  
- Day 2: Frontend (mobile/web UI)  
- Day 3: Testing and demo  

---

## ⚙️ Features
- Ask questions (simulated input)
- Quiz completion system
- Point-based rewards
- Progress tracking (questions, quizzes, points)

---

## 🎯 Vision
To transform screen time into meaningful learning by making curiosity interactive, rewarding, and personalized for every child.
